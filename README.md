## The entry

> Don't let your father's second call ring out on the last train home, the night he says, "Call me back — it's important."
>
> He never called again, and the silence you chose that night became ten years of trying to answer him.

Two lines, each a single sentence. Line one warns the past self about one
specific moment — a second call, the last train home, a sentence said out
loud. Line two traces the cause and effect: the silence chosen that night
became ten years of trying to answer him.

## How it works

`main.ts` writes the entry text to `/output/entry.txt` (exactly two lines,
under the 280-character gate) and prints it to stdout. It self-checks the
gate before writing: exactly two lines, 1–280 characters, not only
whitespace.

Run it with Node 22:

```sh
node main.ts
```
